Delivery information

Current delivery areas and fees are shown in checkout.

Areas and fees

Frostly delivers only to areas currently listed as active in the storefront. Choose your delivery area at checkout to see its fee. If your area is not listed, delivery is not currently available there.

Order confirmation

After you place an order, Frostly reviews and confirms it before preparation. Keep your phone available so Frostly can contact you about your delivery.

Frozen products

Products are packed for frozen delivery. Move them to a freezer promptly after receiving them and follow the product label for storage and preparation.
